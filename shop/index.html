Everything here is something you can actually download and use — open-source designs with models, print settings, and documentation published free on Thingiverse and GitHub. Print them yourself or have someone print them for you. Owners who've built these have written up how it went.

Subaru — SG Forester · 3D-Printed Parts

Precision-fit bumper lights, headlight caps, and interior parts for the SG-generation Forester—printed in ASA, with print projects, CAD source, and install guides.

Amber Auxiliary Light Kit — Assembled Build:

The full build: pre-wired amber LED lights, housings, retainers, and hardware for a plug-and-play installation.

Note:

Requires the factory relay and fog light switch, which you supply yourself.

Free · Open Source

Amber Auxiliary Light Kit — DIY Build:

The parts-only build: housings, retainers, and hardware for those who want to use their own lights.

Note:

Requires the factory relay, fog light switch, your own lights, and the harness connector, all supplied by you.

Free · Open Source
