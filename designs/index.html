Designs

Parts for cars that no longer have a parts catalog. Everything here is something you can download and print — models, print settings, CAD source, and install guides, published free on Thingiverse and GitHub. Owners who've built these have written up how it went.

Subaru — SG Forester · 3D-Printed Parts

Precision-fit bumper lights, headlight caps, and interior parts for the SG-generation Forester — printed in ASA, with print projects, CAD source, and install guides.

Amber Auxiliary Light Kit

Flush-mount amber lights for the SG Sports bumper — the only housing designed specifically for this bumper, with vertical and horizontal articulation to aim the beam. Print the four parts, then either copy the LED pods I used or fit your own light; the page carries the bracket spec your light has to meet, and there's a separate install guide for each path.

Free · CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 · ASA · 2006–2008 Sports

Vented Headlight Bulb Caps

Extended rear caps with louvered vents and an external wire slot, for fitting large LED bulbs with rear cooling fans. The dome clears the fan, the louvers angle down to shed water, and the voltage box rides outside the cap. One model fits both sides.

Free · CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 · ASA · 2006–2008 Forester

Glove Box Organizer Shelf

A one-piece shelf that splits the glove compartment into two levels: the owner's manual and paperwork sit flat on top, the space underneath stays open. Fits the existing compartment with no interior modification and mounts with four bolts.

Free · CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 · ASA · 2006–2008 Forester
